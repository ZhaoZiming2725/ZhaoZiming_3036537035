# Project scope (updated after review)

The final report is `report.pdf`, generated from the current outputs by `code/build_report.py`. This file is a scope note, not an alternative final report.

Research question: did NJ fast-food employment fall relative to eastern PA after the April 1992 minimum-wage increase?

Core replication: Card & Krueger (1994) Tables 2-4 and Figure 1. Distinguish all-available means, 384 paired employment records and the 357-record assigned regression sample. Managers count in FTE; permanent and temporary closures have distinct rules.

Independent question: does company ownership moderate the NJ-PA employment contrast conditional on chain? Use the pre-treatment ownership interaction and HC3 confidence intervals; check sensitivity to the wider employment-only sample. Wage-bite comparisons are already in the original paper and do not alone constitute the independent extension.

Consult the report and REVIEW_NOTES.md for results, validation, source discrepancies and limitations. No remaining task is presented as already completed unless it was actually checked.
