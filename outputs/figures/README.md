# Generated figures

Generated by `code/analysis.py` through the single entry point `python code/run_all.py`.

- `figure1_wage_distribution.png`: replication of the original Figure 1 subject, with NJ/PA starting-wage distributions before and after the increase. Bin definitions and denominators are exported in `outputs/tables/figure1_wage_bins.csv`.
- `fig_extension_ownership.png`: conditional employment-change contrasts by ownership and their ownership difference, with 95% HC3 intervals.

Both images are inserted automatically into the PDF/HTML report. The original Figure 1 is a wage-distribution figure; a mean-employment chart cannot substitute for it. See the report for binning and inference limitations.
